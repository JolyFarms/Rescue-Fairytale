About the project

A place where every
life matters

Fairy-tale Sanctuary has been healing, harbouring and rehabilitating rescued farm and wild animals for over 15 years. In this time Fairy-tale was a safe and nourishing home to several hundred animals.

Currently it is caring for over 70 beautiful animals from Slovenia and other countries in Europe. We all coexist and depend on each other and on animals’ and nature’s wellbeing. Nobody can exist and survive alone, that’s why we live as a society. What kind of society?

“The true measure of any society can be found in how it treats it’s most vulnerable members,” - Mahatma Gandhi.

Animals are our silent witnesses and inspiration with their pure emotions and feelings, void of any selfish, hatefull and hypocritical behaviours. We can learn sooooo much from them.

All the animals and the Sanctuary need your help. We need to secure their future in two steps:

1)buying the property and renovating the enclosers / existing buildings, total estimate cost: 1.000.000 €, urgently needed, matter of days

2)expanding the Sanctuary, buying additional land, build facilities for storage, education, food preparation, medical help and living quarters for more animals and guests, total estimate cost: 12.000.000 €.
